Packing guide

  • Let cooked food cool, then seal it within 2 hours of cooking.
  • One meal per container. Wrap the box so it stays closed.
  • Mark nuts, dairy, eggs, shellfish, and pork clearly.
  • Put cooked food in a fridge point if the box says “Refrigerated”.
  • Drop it off during open hours and list it right away.
